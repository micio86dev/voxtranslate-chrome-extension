## Detailed description

```
VoxTranslate translates the audio playing in your browser tab and gives you live
subtitles over the page plus a spoken translation you can mix against the original.

Because it works on the tab's audio rather than on any one service's API, it covers
whatever is making sound in Chrome:

• Google Meet and Zoom on the WEB — their browser clients, not the desktop apps
• YouTube videos and live streams
• Twitch
• Online courses and lecture players
• Podcasts and webinar rooms
• Anything else in a tab that plays audio

WHAT YOU GET

• Live subtitles in your language, drawn over the page and following the video into
  fullscreen. Adjust size and position, or show the original line underneath.
• Spoken translation on the tiers that produce audio.
• A single 0–100% control over the ORIGINAL audio. While the translated voice speaks,
  the original ducks underneath it and comes straight back when it stops — so you keep
  the speaker's timing and tone instead of losing them.
• Your language chosen per session. The spoken language is detected for you.
• Live usage and remaining balance in the side panel, so nothing is a surprise.

PERMISSIONS, AND WHY

VoxTranslate does not request access to your browsing.

It uses activeTab, so pressing the toolbar button grants access to that ONE tab and
nothing else. There is no <all_urls> permission, and no access to your tabs, history,
cookies or network requests.

It captures the audio of the single tab you started it on. Never your microphone, never
another tab, never a background tab. The page itself is never sent — not the URL, not the
title, not the video id, not the page content. What leaves your browser is the audio, the
language you chose, and your session token.

WHAT IT DOES NOT DO

• Chrome only. Firefox, Safari and Edge are not supported.
• The web clients of Google Meet and Zoom, not their desktop applications — it listens to
  a browser tab, and a desktop app does not have one.
• It translates what you HEAR. It does not translate you for the other participants.
• Chrome forbids injecting scripts into its own pages and the Web Store, so subtitles do
  not appear there. Audio translation still runs.
• The spoken language is detected once when you press start, so a video that switches
  language halfway through needs the session restarted.
• We do not publish a latency figure. We have not measured translation delay on the
  shipped build, and it varies with the tier, the language pair, your device and your
  network.

LANGUAGES

Coverage depends on the tier you pick, and the picker shows exactly which languages each
one offers. The Standard tier covers 29 languages; the Premium tier covers all 84 in the
VoxTranslate catalogue.

ACCOUNT AND BILLING

Requires a VoxTranslate account. Translation is billed per minute of speech from your
account credit — it is not a subscription, and unused credit does not expire monthly.
New accounts start with free credit and no card.

A NOTE ON AI

Subtitles and any translated voice are generated by AI and can contain errors. The
translated voice is synthesized — it is not a recording of the speaker. VoxTranslate is
built for everyday comprehension, not for legal, medical or safety-critical decisions.
```

## Why the copy is shaped this way

**"WHAT IT DOES NOT DO" is not a disclaimer section, it is the middle of the listing.**
The two things most likely to produce a one-star review are someone installing it for the
Zoom *desktop* app, and someone expecting it to translate them for the other participants.
Both are stated plainly, above the fold of the description, in the reviewer's own reading
order. A refund is more expensive than a lost install.

**No superlatives.** Not "the best", not "instant", not "accurate". The product is good at
a specific thing and the listing describes that thing.

**Per-tier language counts.** 84 is the size of the catalogue and the reach of one tier.
Writing "84 languages" flat would be the same overstatement the marketing site carried
until it was corrected — see `docs/marketing/directory-listings.md:17-18` in the parent
workspace.
